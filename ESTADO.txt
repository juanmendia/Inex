3. DECISIONES DE ARQUITECTURA
==================================================

Stack
- Next.js App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind + shadcn/ui + Lucide.
- PostgreSQL + Supabase Auth + Storage + RLS.
- Zod en server. Server Actions para mutaciones internas; Route Handlers solo si hace falta (signed URL, cron, webhooks).
- i18n: textos en español, keys listas para después (no i18n lib en MVP salvo que salga gratis).

Multi-tenant
- Un schema, columna tenant_id en casi todo.
- RLS: auth.uid() → profile.tenant_id (SUPER_ADMIN bypass solo tablas de plataforma, no receipts/employees salvo permiso explícito).
- Storage path: tenants/{tenant_id}/...
- Middleware: sesión + rol → layout correcto. Nunca filtrar solo en UI.

Documentos vs recibos
- files: blob metadata (bucket, path, mime, size, checksum).
- documents: contenedor (type, employee_id nullable, status, current_file_id).
- document_versions: historial de archivos.
- receipts: 1:1 con document (period, type, payroll_run_id nullable, due_at).
- receipt_signatures: action CONFORM | NON_CONFORM, ip, ua, hash, reason.

Módulos (carpetas, no microservicios)
  core: auth, tenants, users, rbac, files, documents, notifications, audit
  hr: employees, receipts, tickets, events, announcements, birthdays (derivado de employees)

Engines futuros (interfaces vacías + tablas)
  AttendanceEngine, PayrollEngine — no lógica.

Seguridad
- Sin service_role en cliente.
- Signed URLs cortas.
- IDOR: receipt.id no alcanza; join employee + tenant + (owner o rol HR).
- Soft delete / status, no DELETE físico de datos críticos.
- audit_logs: insert-only para roles normales.

Auth flow
  login → session → profile (tenant_id, role) → redirect
    EMPLOYEE → /app
    HR_* / TENANT_ADMIN → /rrhh
    SUPER_ADMIN → /admin

4. MODELO DE DATOS (MVP + stubs)
==================================================

Enums
  tenant_status: active | suspended | cancelled
  user_status: active | disabled
  employee_status: active | inactive | suspended
  document_type: receipt | contract | certificate | policy | notice | other
  document_status: pending | available | signed | non_conforming | cancelled
  receipt_status: pending | signed | non_conforming | expired | cancelled
  ticket_status: open | in_progress | answered | closed
  ticket_category: receipt | salary | vacation | leave | documents | health | personal | other
  event_type: training | meeting | internal | anniversary | holiday | other
  announcement_audience: company | department | employees
  birthday_visibility: public | department | private
  audit_action: login | logout | create | update | delete | view | download | sign | sign_non_conform | upload | publish | answer
  role_code: super_admin | tenant_admin | hr_admin | hr_operator | employee
  payroll_run_status: draft | in_review | approved | closed | cancelled   (stub)
  novelty_status: pending | approved | rejected | liquidated               (stub)
  attendance_method: mobile_gps | qr | web                                 (stub)

Tablas CORE
  tenants
  tenant_settings (logo, colors, timezone, birthday_visibility, receipt config)
  profiles (id=auth.users.id, tenant_id nullable para super_admin, status, email)
  roles / permissions / role_permissions / user_roles
  files
  documents / document_versions
  notifications
  audit_logs (append-only)

Tablas HR
  departments / positions
  employees (tenant_id, user_id, employee_number, dni, birth_date, hire_date, …)
  receipts / receipt_signatures / receipt_non_conformities
  hr_tickets / hr_ticket_messages
  events
  announcements / announcement_targets

Stubs (crear en migración 1, sin UI)
  collective_agreements, agreement_versions, salary_scales, salary_scale_items
  employee_agreements, employee_categories
  payroll_concepts, payroll_rules, payroll_runs, payroll_run_employees, payroll_items, payroll_novelties
  work_locations, work_schedules, work_schedule_days, employee_work_schedules
  attendance_records, attendance_events

Reglas
  PK uuid. FK + índices (tenant_id, employee_id, period, status).
  Unique (tenant_id, employee_number), (tenant_id, dni) where not deleted.
  Todo recurso de negocio con tenant_id. Soft status, no hard delete.
